Who we are

Built from a small
beginning in 1978.

A textile story that started with one man and a block of carved wood, and grew into a family-run manufacturing house.

The founder

Harish B. Trivedi began with block printing.

In 1978, at around 22 years old, Harish B. Trivedi started the textile journey with hand block printing. His wife, Damyati Trivedi, was beside him in those early years — learning the fabric, the colours and the trade together.

We keep the old photographs on this site on purpose. They are evidence of where the business actually came from.
