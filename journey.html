Our journey

1978
to today.

Told through the photographs the family kept — from a single block table to a factory floor of two hundred hands at peak strength.

1978

The first block

Harish B. Trivedi begins the textile journey with hand block printing, at around 22 years of age, with his wife Damyati beside him.

EARLY ERA

Learning the craft

Printing, colour mixing, fabric knowledge and client relationships grow through hands-on work at the first small workshop near Mahalaxmi.

NEXT GEN.

Vikram & Vishal join

The business expands beyond block printing into screen printing, embroidery, digital printing and broader manufacturing capability.

GROWTH

Piyush joins the production flow

Departmental programming and production coordination become part of the growing factory operation.

TODAY

A wholesale manufacturing house

Design through finished packing, with printing, embroidery, value addition and QC running across the workflow.
